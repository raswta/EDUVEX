EDUVEX Complete Operational Guide 💡

1. Goal Setting & Saving: Select Daily or Weekly goals, type your target hours, and click Save Goal. Exceeding your target keeps the progress bar locked at 100% full without breaking.

2. Unified Subject Lists & Auto-Save: Subjects selected in the Stopwatch, Manual Entry, Exams, and Classes are automatically synchronized. Choose "+ Add New Subject" to type and store new subject names permanently.

3. Manual & Edited Manual Records: Any manual study entry instantly triggers the Edited Manual tag, coloring that entire day's bar orange with an explicit badge.

4. Study Room & Refresh: Use the dedicated Refresh Room button or auto-sync features to check active members. Toggle the Anonymous checkbox before joining to show or hide your real username.

5. Chart Layers & Stacked Breakdown: Charts feature a max scale of 20 hours. You can toggle Classes, Stacked Breakdown by Subject, and Exam Grades Overlay independently.

6. Weekdays Order: Weekly charts and selectors strictly follow the Saturday to Friday (Shanbeh to Jomeh) cycle.
