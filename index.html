EDUVEX User Guide 💡

1. Accounts: Sign up with any username and password. Your data securely syncs across all your devices.

2. Stopwatch & Rest Timer: Track live sessions accurately. Notifications will stay active even if you switch tabs. Auto-rest timer tracks breaks when you return.

3. Exams & Classes: Add upcoming exams with countdowns and input your weekly class schedule which automatically integrates into study hours.

4. Study Room: Join the shared room to compare stopwatch study hours with friends in real-time. Only live stopwatch sessions count here!

5. Charts & Colors: Stopwatch sessions appear in Blue, Classes in Green, and Manual entries in Orange with an "Edited / Manual" label.
