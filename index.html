Turn on reminders?

If you forget that the stopwatch is running, EDUVEX will send you a message after 10 minutes and again after 30 minutes to tell you it is still on. After that, no more messages. It works even when the app is closed.

iPhone / iPad: this only works if you added EDUVEX to your Home Screen and opened it from that icon.

EDUVEX Complete Guide

Illustrated step-by-step guide (with pictures): t.me/EDUVEXtut

Goal bar: choose Daily or Weekly, type your target hours (12.5 means 12 hours 30 minutes) and press Save Goal. The bar counts your study sessions plus your weekly classes.

Streak: the character next to the goal bar counts your days in a row. It is angry until you start studying today, and it glows when you reach your daily goal. Tap it to choose a weekly day off that never breaks your streak.

⚔️ Duel: challenge any user for today up to 3 days ahead. Yesterday's duel result stays visible for up to 12 hours after completion (at 3:00 AM if both were studying, or immediately when both finish).
