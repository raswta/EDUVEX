EDUVEX Complete Guide

Illustrated step-by-step guide (with pictures): t.me/EDUVEXtut

Goal bar: choose Daily or Weekly, type your target hours (12.5 means 12 hours 30 minutes) and press Save Goal. The bar counts your study sessions plus your weekly classes.

Streak: the character next to the goal bar counts your days in a row. It is angry until you start studying today, and it glows when you reach your daily goal. Tap it to choose a weekly day off that never breaks your streak.

Streak character: the character shows your level. Angry: you have not started studying today (with any streak). Calm: you started today and have a 1-14 day streak. Sparkle: 15 days. Black graduation cap: 30 days. Red graduation cap: 60 days. Tap it to see all levels.

Leaderboard: in the Study Room tab, everyone is ranked by streak (most days first), with their username, streak and level character. Everyone with a streak is listed automatically.

Timer: pick a subject and press Start. To study another subject, press Pause, choose the new subject and press Resume. The big clock keeps the total, and each subject is saved with its own time when you press Finish & Save. Sessions shorter than 15 seconds are not saved. The stopwatch keeps going if you refresh the page.

Auto-save at 3:00 AM: if you paused the stopwatch and forgot to press Finish & Save, it is saved automatically at 3:00 AM (or when you next open the app) on the day you studied, so your streak is not lost.

Still studying? check: if the stopwatch runs for 3 hours without you touching the app, a popup asks whether you are still studying. Tap Yes within 10 minutes to keep going. If you do not, the stopwatch pauses and the waiting time is not counted. For very long sessions, use Focus mode so the screen stays on and you can answer.

Focus mode: tap the expand icon at the top to show only a big study clock and a small Study Room window. Nothing is locked, so you can leave the app any time. Press Exit focus mode at the bottom to come back.

Pomodoro: tick the box, set study and rest minutes, and the clock counts down. Rest time is not added to your study time.

Manual entry: add minutes you studied without the stopwatch, for any date (including a reason/explanation). In the charts these keep their subject color with a thick yellow border.

Logs: every saved session appears in Saved Study Logs. Use the trash icon to delete one. Items that say pending sync were saved offline and will upload automatically.

Exams & Classes: add exams to see the days left. Weekly classes repeat every week and count toward your goal and the Classes layer in the charts.

Tests: in the Tests tab pick a day, type how many practice tests you solved for each subject (optional) and press Save tests. The study time of that day is shown next to each subject, and you can edit the numbers any time. The chart shows tests by subject for one day, or day by day for the whole week. Subjects keep their colors. You can also write a Topic (optional) in the Timer card and press Set (or Enter); it is saved with the session and shown in the PDF summary. Under the timer you see the time of each topic.

To-Do: add tasks for today, tick them, edit them with the pen or delete them. The list is kept only on this device and is deleted when the day ends. In the Daily summary (More tab) you can tick "Include today's to-do list" to add it to the PDF; it is off by default.

Study Room: automatically joins when your timer starts and leaves when you finish. Users with 30+ day streaks unlock manual join/leave controls.

Charts: the weekly chart runs Saturday to Friday and the monthly chart shows every day of the month. Use Review Archive to open previous weeks and months. Stacked shows hours per subject, Total shows one line. Log exam scores to see them on the monthly chart.

Summary PDF: open the More tab, choose Daily, Weekly or Monthly, pick the period (This week, Last month, a date...) and tick Include classes if you want class hours added. Press Preview summary, then Download PDF. It shows manual reasons under each manual entry, study time, tests, hours per subject with topics, and charts. You need internet the first time you download.

Settings (gear icon): switch all dates between Shamsi and Gregorian, rename or delete subjects, add new subjects, turn reminders on or off, send a test notification, change your password, and log out. If you delete a subject, its name disappears but the time you already studied stays in the charts under Other.

Reminders: if you forget the stopwatch is running, you get a message after 10 minutes and again after 30 minutes, even when the app is closed. After that, nothing more. Pausing or finishing cancels them.

Offline: everything you add without internet is saved on your device and uploads when you are back online. Subject changes and the study room need internet.

Home Screen app: after you add EDUVEX to your Home Screen, updates arrive by themselves the next time you open it. You do not need to add it again.
