EDUVEX Complete Guide

Goal bar: choose Daily or Weekly, type your target hours (12.5 means 12 hours 30 minutes) and press Save Goal. The bar counts your study sessions plus your weekly classes.

Timer: pick a subject and press Start. To study another subject, press Pause, choose the new subject and press Resume. The big clock keeps the total, and each subject is saved with its own time when you press Finish & Save. Sessions shorter than 15 seconds are not saved. The stopwatch keeps going if you refresh the page.

Pomodoro: tick the box, set study and rest minutes, and the clock counts down. Rest time is not added to your study time.

Manual entry: add minutes you studied without the stopwatch, for any date. In the charts these keep their subject color with a thick yellow border.

Logs: every saved session appears in Saved Study Logs. Use the trash icon to delete one. Items that say pending sync were saved offline and will upload automatically.

Exams & Classes: add exams to see the days left. Weekly classes repeat every week and count toward your goal and the Classes layer in the charts.

Tests: in the Tests tab pick a day, type how many practice tests you solved for each subject (optional) and press Save tests. The study time of that day is shown next to each subject, and you can edit the numbers any time. The chart shows tests by subject for one day, or day by day for the whole week. Subjects keep their colors. You can also write a Topic (optional) in the Timer card; it is saved with the session and shown in the PDF summary.

Study Room: start your timer, then press Join Room to show your live study time to others. You can join as Anonymous. Rooms need internet.

Charts: the weekly chart runs Saturday to Friday and the monthly chart shows every day of the month. Use Review Archive to open previous weeks and months. Stacked shows hours per subject, Total shows one line. Log exam scores to see them on the monthly chart.

Summary PDF: open the More tab, choose Daily, Weekly or Monthly, pick the period (This week, Last month, a date...) and tick Include classes if you want class hours added. Press Preview summary, then Download PDF. It shows study time, tests, hours per subject with topics, and charts. You need internet the first time you download.

Settings (gear icon): switch all dates between Shamsi and Gregorian, rename or delete subjects, add new subjects, turn reminders on or off, and log out. If you delete a subject, its name disappears but the time you already studied stays in the charts under Other.

Reminders: if you forget the stopwatch is running, you get a message after 10 minutes and again after 30 minutes, even when the app is closed. After that, nothing more. Pausing or finishing cancels them.

Offline: everything you add without internet is saved on your device and uploads when you are back online. Subject changes and the study room need internet.

Home Screen app: after you add EDUVEX to your Home Screen, updates arrive by themselves the next time you open it. You do not need to add it again.
