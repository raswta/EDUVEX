EDUVEX Complete Operational Guide 💡

1. Goal Setting & Saving: Select Daily or Weekly goals, type your target hours (supports decimals like 12.5 for 12 hours and 30 mins), and click Save Goal.

2. Changing Subjects & Timer: To change your study subject, simply pause the timer, select a new subject from the dropdown, or continue with "General Study" from the very beginning. As soon as you click Finish & Save, your session time is instantly recorded and added to that day's charts and logs!

3. Classes & Manual Entries: Both manual entries and scheduled classes are immediately synced and added to your daily/weekly charts upon saving.

4. Persistent Stopwatch: Your stopwatch never resets when you refresh or leave the tab.
