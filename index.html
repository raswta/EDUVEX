EDUVEX Guide

Goal: choose Daily or Weekly, type hours (12.5 = 12h30m) and press Save Goal.

Timer: Finish & Save records the session in your logs, goal bar and charts. The stopwatch survives refresh.

Classes & manual entries are added to goal and charts immediately.

Archive: pick any previous week or month in the Charts tab.

Calendar: open Settings (gear icon) to switch all dates between Shamsi and Gregorian, edit subjects, or log out.

Yellow border: in the charts, hours added with Manual Entry keep their subject color but have a thick yellow border.
