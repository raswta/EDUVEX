EDUVEX Complete User Guide 💡

1. Accounts & Security: Sign up with any username and password. Make sure to keep your credentials safe because password recovery is not supported.

2. Live Stopwatch & Pomodoro: Go to Timer & Study, enter your subject name, and hit Start. If you enable Pomodoro Mode, you can configure your custom study and rest minutes. When time expires, a fullscreen alert will notify you instantly.

3. Manual Time Entry (How-to): If you studied without the live timer, use the Manual Time Entry box right next to the stopwatch. Just type the subject name, enter the total minutes you studied (e.g., 60), and click Save Manual Entry. These entries will show up with an orange badge and an "Edited / Manual" label in your logs and charts.

4. Exams & Classes: Add upcoming exams to view live countdowns. Add your weekly class schedule; class durations automatically integrate into your total study hours and charts.

5. Study Room: Join the shared room to compare stopwatch study hours with others. (Note: Manual entries and classes are excluded from the room leaderboard).

6. Charts & Colors: Stopwatch sessions are Blue, Classes are Green, and Manual entries are Orange.
