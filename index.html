EDUVEX Complete Guide 💡

1. General Stopwatch & Live Subjects: Click Start to begin your continuous study timer. You can freely change your subject name in the input box anytime without restarting or losing your elapsed time.

2. Pomodoro & Rest Timers: If Pomodoro mode is enabled, your study session runs (e.g. 50 mins) and then automatically switches to a rest countdown. During rest breaks, your study hours pause.

3. Live Study Room & Badges: In the Live Room, active study sessions display a vibrant blue 👨‍💻 icon. If you switch to a rest break or pause, the icon turns grayscale and dimmed. Your live stopwatch time streams to room members in real-time.

4. Saving & Leaderboard Rule: Scores and saved hours are **only** added to your profile when you click Finish & Save (which prompts a confirmation warning to prevent accidental clicks). Pausing does not save or publish scores. The final leaderboard feature is currently coming soon!

5. Manual Entry: Use the manual entry box in the Timer tab to record hours studied offline quickly.
